# AI Usage Declaration

**Tools Used:**
- AI Coding Assistant (Antigravity/Gemini)

**Description of Use:**
I utilized an AI coding assistant during this assignment primarily as a brainstorming and design validation partner. Specifically, I used the AI for:

1. **Understanding Design & Architecture:** The assistant helped me analyze the detailed pros and cons of my validator's architecture, specifically when comparing the limitations of programmatic deterministic verifiers versus the flexibility and failure modes of Vision-Language Models (VLMs).

2. **Brainstorming Validations:** The AI assisted me in brainstorming more granular validation test cases to catch edge-case errors. For example, it helped me formulate the explicit itemized checklists and "Chain of Thought" extraction techniques used to improve the `wrong_chart` and `hard_to_read` validation functions.

3. **Report Generation:** I used the assistant to help compile, format, and structure my evaluation metrics into the final LaTeX report.
